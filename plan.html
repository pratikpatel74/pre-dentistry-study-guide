Year 12 study plan

Week by week from November 2026 to the UCAS deadline in October 2027: untimed drills, then timed sets, then full mocks and the official UCAT practice tests, around A-level work and your application.

Keep it realistic. A-level grades matter more than anything else this year, so UCAT stays small until the summer. Swap weeks around to fit your school's holidays and exams; the order matters more than the dates. Dates for UCAT 2027 and UCAS 2028 entry are published in spring 2027, so check them then.

The phases

2 Nov 2026 – 20 Dec 2026
Groundwork

Year 12 grades come first: they drive your predicted grades. Learn what the UCAT is, line up work experience and start a regular volunteering role.

Each week: A-levels as normal · UCAT: one 20–30 minute untimed drill a week · one application task a week
21 Dec 2026 – 3 Jan 2027
Christmas break

Rest, catch up on A-level work and keep UCAT ticking over.

Each week: One drill and one reading task a week
4 Jan 2027 – 28 Mar 2027
Build the skills

Learn every UCAT question type properly with untimed drills, using New set for fresh Decision Making and Quantitative Reasoning questions.

Each week: UCAT: two 30-minute sessions a week · quick mental maths and fast reading on other days
29 Mar 2027 – 11 Apr 2027
Easter

Ideal time for dental work experience. Check the UCAT 2027 dates and register when it opens.

Each week: Work experience if booked · first timed sets
12 Apr 2027 – 23 May 2027
Timed practice

Move to timed sets at real pace. Review every mistake: the review is where the marks come from.

Each week: UCAT: three timed sets a week, plus a review of mistakes
24 May 2027 – 4 Jul 2027
Exam season

Year 12 exams matter for your predictions, so UCAT drops to maintenance. Book your test date if you haven't already.

Each week: UCAT: one or two timed sets a week · revise for exams
5 Jul 2027 – 8 Aug 2027
UCAT intensive

Four to five weeks of daily practice: full mocks and the official UCAT practice tests. Sit the test in late July or early August so September is free for your application.

Each week: UCAT: 45–90 minutes a day, 5–6 days a week · one full mock a week
9 Aug 2027 – 17 Oct 2027
Application

Use your UCAT result to choose four dental schools, finish the personal statement and submit before the deadline. Start interview practice.

Each week: Personal statement drafts · school research · interview practice from September
