Chemistry to know cold

Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆(OH)₂ + 8H⁺ ⇌ 10Ca²⁺ + 6HPO₄²⁻ + 2H₂O
Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆(OH)₂ + 2F⁻ → Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆F₂ + 2OH⁻
  • Enamel dissolves below ~pH 5.5; fluorapatite ~4.5; root dentine ~6.2–6.7.
  • pH is logarithmic: pH 4.5 is 10× more acidic than 5.5.

Worked maths: local anaesthetic

2% = 2 g per 100 mL = 20 mg/mL. A 2.2 mL cartridge holds 2.2 × 20 = 44 mg. Maximum doses are per kg of body weight, so practise mg/kg sums. Learn the official maximum doses from your course.
