Professionalism & Ethics

The GDC's Standards for the Dental Team set nine principles that apply to students as well as registered professionals.

The nine principles

  1. Put patients' interests first.
  2. Communicate effectively with patients.
  3. Obtain valid consent.
  4. Maintain and protect patients' information.
  5. Have a clear and effective complaints procedure.
  6. Work with colleagues in a way that is in patients' best interests.
  7. Maintain, develop and work within your professional knowledge and skills.
  8. Raise concerns if patients are at risk.
  9. Make sure your personal behaviour maintains patients' confidence in you and the dental profession.

Watch for change: in 2026 the GDC consulted on replacing these with a Framework for Professionalism built on four principles. Revised Standards for Education apply from 2026/27. Check the GDC site for the current version.

Valid consent

  • Informed: options (including none), risks, benefits, costs
  • Voluntary: no pressure
  • Capacity: understand, retain, weigh up, communicate (Mental Capacity Act 2005)
  • Ongoing: can be withdrawn at any time
  • Children: under-16s can consent if Gillick competent

Ethics basics

  • Four principles: autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, justice
  • Confidentiality: share only with consent, by law, or to prevent serious harm (UK GDPR)
  • Duty of candour: be open when things go wrong
  • Safeguarding: know how to raise concerns
  • Social media: can trigger fitness-to-practise proceedings
