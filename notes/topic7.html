Infection Control & Occupational Health

Dental students carry out exposure-prone procedures, so every UK dental school requires blood-borne virus screening and hepatitis B vaccination before you can treat patients.

Before you start

  • Hepatitis B vaccine: usually 0, 1 and 6 months, then a blood test to confirm immunity. Some schools want you to start before arrival; others arrange it after. Check your school's instructions.
  • Screening: hepatitis B, hepatitis C and HIV.
  • Other checks: TB/BCG, MMR, chickenpox, occupational health questionnaire.
  • Enhanced DBS check.

Core infection control

  1. Standard precautions: every patient is potentially infectious
  2. Hand hygiene: the most effective measure
  3. Decontamination: clean → inspect → sterilise (autoclave) → store; England follows HTM 01-05
  4. Sharps injury: bleed it, wash it, cover it, report it
  5. Waste: colour-coded bags and sharps bins

Safety

  • Radiation: IR(ME)R regulations; ALARP
  • Emergencies: fainting (most common), angina, asthma, anaphylaxis, hypoglycaemia, seizures, cardiac arrest. Annual BLS training.
