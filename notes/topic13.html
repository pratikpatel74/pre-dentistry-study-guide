Dental Radiography

X-rays let you see decay between teeth, root problems and bone loss. Every exposure is ionising radiation, so it must be justified and kept as low as reasonably practicable.

Radiation protection

  • IR(ME)R roles: referrer requests, practitioner justifies, operator takes the image. One dentist can be all three.
  • Justification: benefit must outweigh risk. Optimisation: keep dose as low as reasonably practicable (ALARP).
  • Inverse square law: double the distance, quarter the dose.
  • Rectangular collimation and digital sensors reduce dose.
  • Pregnancy isn't a bar to a justified dental X-ray.
  • Every image must be reported (clinically evaluated) and recorded.
