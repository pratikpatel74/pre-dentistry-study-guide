The UK Dental Landscape

UK dentistry is a regulated profession. Every dentist must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC), and patients are treated through the NHS or privately.

The BDS course (typical shape)

  1. Years 1–2: biomedical sciences (anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, oral biology), phantom-head skills, early patient contact.
  2. Years 3–4: treating patients under supervision: fillings, extractions, gum care, root canals, crowns, dentures, children's dentistry.
  3. Year 5: consolidation, outreach clinics, finals.
  4. After graduating: one year of Dental Foundation Training (DFT) in an NHS practice.

Courses vary by university, so check your chosen school's handbook.
