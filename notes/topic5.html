Periodontal (Gum) Disease

Gum disease is driven by plaque bacteria and the body's inflammatory response. Gingivitis is reversible; periodontitis destroys bone and is not.

The periodontium

  • Gingiva: gum around the neck of the tooth
  • Periodontal ligament: collagen fibres from cementum to bone
  • Cementum: covers the root
  • Alveolar bone: the socket

Gingivitis vs periodontitis

GingivitisPeriodontitis
AffectsGum onlyGum, ligament and bone
SignsRed, swollen gums that bleedPockets > 3 mm, recession, bone loss, loose teeth
Reversible?YesNo (can be stabilised)
Risk factorsPlaquePlaque + smoking, diabetes, genetics, stress

Key terms

  • Pocket: gap deeper than the normal 1–3 mm sulcus
  • Calculus: mineralised plaque; professional removal only
  • BPE: score 0–4 (plus *) per sextant at every check-up
  • Systemic links: diabetes (two-way) and cardiovascular disease
