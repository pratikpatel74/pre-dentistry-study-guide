Oral Pathology & Oral Cancer

Dentists see the mouth more often than any other clinician, so they're often first to spot oral cancer. Know the common harmless conditions and the red flags.

Common conditions

Oral cancer

  • About 9 in 10 are squamous cell carcinomas.
  • Risk factors: tobacco and alcohol (worse together), betel quid / paan / areca nut, HPV (oropharynx), age, poor diet.
  • Common sites: side of the tongue, floor of the mouth.
  • Potentially malignant disorders: leukoplakia (white patch), erythroplakia (red patch, higher risk), some lichen planus.

Referral criteria follow NICE NG12 (suspected cancer recognition and referral).
