Your Route to 2028 Entry

Applications for September 2028 open in summer 2027 and close in mid-October 2027. That gives Year 12 one year to build grades, experience and a strong UCAT.

Key facts

ItemWhat to know
UCAS deadlineMid-October 2027 (15 October in recent years, 6pm). Check UCAS dates when published.
ChoicesUp to 4 dentistry; the 5th can be a backup course in another subject
A-levelsUsually AAA incl. Chemistry and Biology; some ask A*AA
UCATSit once in summer 2027 (2026 window: 13 July to 24 September)
Work experienceE.g. Birmingham: usually at least 3 days in an NHS or mixed practice, plus caring/volunteering
InterviewsRoughly December to March; MMI or panel, depending on the school
ResultsA-level results August 2028; start September 2028

Choosing schools

  • Typical offers and interview formats: most schools ask AAA; KCL and Queen Mary typically A*AA. About 8 use MMIs and about 4 panel interviews; some change format, so check each year (summary).
  • Schools weight GCSEs, UCAT and interviews differently. Apply where your profile is strongest.
  • Some schools won't consider you if you also apply for medicine (e.g. Birmingham).
  • Check contextual offer schemes if you may be eligible.

Year 12 checklist

  • ☐ Strong Year 12 grades (they drive predictions)
  • ☐ Book dental practice shadowing and a regular volunteering role
  • ☐ Keep a reflective diary of every placement
  • ☐ Register for the UCAT when it opens (spring 2027) and book an early summer slot
  • ☐ Attend open days and shortlist 4 schools
  • ☐ Draft the personal statement over the summer
