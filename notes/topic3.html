Tooth Types, Notation & Eruption

Adults have 32 permanent teeth and children have 20 primary teeth. UK dental schools use the two-digit FDI system to name each one.

First digit = quadrant (1–4 permanent, 5–8 primary). Second digit = position counted from the midline. Say digits separately: "one-six", not "sixteen".

Palmer notation is still seen in UK orthodontics: a grid symbol shows the quadrant, with 1–8 for permanent teeth and A–E for primary teeth.
